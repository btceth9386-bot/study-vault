# Highlights

- `p. 12-14, Section: The spectrum: vibe coding to agentic engineering` AI-assisted development is a spectrum distinguished by the amount of structure, verification, and human judgment surrounding model output; the correct level depends on the stakes.
- `p. 14-15, Section: Context engineering: the real skill` Deterministic tests verify exact behavior, while evaluations assess non-deterministic qualities such as tool choice, trajectory, and response quality.
- `p. 15-18, Section: Context engineering: the real skill` Effective agents receive six forms of context— instructions, knowledge, memory, examples, tools, and guardrails—split deliberately between always-loaded and on-demand material.
- `p. 19-20, Section: The traditional SDLC under pressure` AI compresses implementation much faster than requirements, architecture, and verification, shifting the bottleneck from typing code to defining boundaries and judging results.
- `p. 24-25, Section: The factory model` The developer's primary output becomes the system that produces code: specifications, agents, quality gates, feedback loops, and guardrails.
- `p. 26-30, Section: Harness Engineering` An operational agent is a model plus a harness of rules, tools, execution environments, orchestration, hooks, and observability that remains active throughout the SDLC.
- `p. 32-34, Section: The developer's evolving role` Developers move between conductor mode for hands-on direction and orchestrator mode for asynchronous delegation, decomposition, and review.
- `p. 34, Section: The 80% problem` AI can generate the obvious bulk quickly, but edge cases, integrations, ambiguous requirements, and architectural correctness still demand deep contextual judgment.
- `p. 39-42, Section: The Economics of AI Development` Vibe coding trades low upfront investment for compounding token, maintenance, and remediation costs; agentic engineering makes the reverse trade through reusable context and verification infrastructure.
- `p. 47-48, Section: Conclusion: Intent as the new Interface` Structure scales while vibes do not, AI amplifies the engineering culture it enters, and human work shifts toward verification, judgment, and direction.
