# Highlights

- `p. 3` Enterprise AI transformation follows three stages: establish the foundation, launch carefully chosen pilots, and scale proven impact.
- `p. 5` High-impact use cases begin with current business pain and sustainable improvement, not an attempt to transform every function at once.
- `pp. 5-6` A steering committee combines executive authority, operational knowledge, technical expertise, financial accountability, and legal or compliance leadership, while local champions surface practical concerns and encourage adoption.
- `pp. 6-7` Pilot success should be measured across adoption, efficiency, quality, and satisfaction, with an 8–12 week time box and recurring reviews that turn data into decisions.
- `p. 7` Governance should define access controls, acceptable-use boundaries, output-quality thresholds, human-review requirements, and jurisdiction-specific compliance protocols.
- `p. 9` Initial pilots should balance expected ROI, technical feasibility, user receptivity, process disruption, resource requirements, and the business impact of failure.
- `pp. 9-10` Sprint-based pilots can show value in 30–60 days; cross-functional showcases spread successful patterns, while scope discipline protects the learning timeline.
- `p. 10` Pilot post-mortems should combine metrics with unexpected benefits, workarounds, adoption resistance, reliability issues, integration friction, and data-quality surprises.
- `pp. 12-13` Scaling requires audience-specific training, demonstrated proficiency, and a cross-functional center of excellence supported by temporary rotations from operating teams.
- `p. 13` Enterprise ROI includes financial savings, productivity, time-to-market, decision quality, and employee experience; quantified results become more persuasive when paired with concrete stories.
- `pp. 15-16` Anthropic's internal examples show the largest gains when teams redesign end-to-end workflows across engineering, sales, legal, marketing, finance, and HR instead of automating isolated tasks.
- `p. 18` Readiness should be assessed across executive commitment, infrastructure, technical capability, change management, collaboration, AI maturity, risk controls, and sustained funding.
