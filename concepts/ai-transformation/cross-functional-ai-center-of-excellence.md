# Cross-Functional AI Center of Excellence

- **One-sentence definition**: A cross-functional AI center of excellence is an accountable team that develops reusable practices, supports implementations, experiments, and transfers knowledge across functions.
- **Why it exists / what problem it solves**: Without a shared team, each department relearns the same integration and governance lessons.
- **Keywords**: best practices, support, experimentation, rotation, knowledge transfer
- **Related concepts**: [[agent-specialization-as-scaling-mechanism]]
- **Depth**: 2/4
- **Last updated**: 2026-09-26
- **Source**: The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide

## Summary

A center of excellence turns isolated pilot lessons into reusable organizational capability. It combines technical architects, domain experts, and data specialists to support teams and test new use cases. Rotating functional experts through it keeps central guidance connected to real operations.

## Example

After two teams build similar document assistants, the center publishes a shared data-access checklist, offers troubleshooting hours, and rotates a finance analyst into the team for three months.
