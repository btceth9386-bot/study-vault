# Enterprise AI Transformation Stages

- **One-sentence definition**: Enterprise AI transformation stages move an organization from building foundations, to testing focused pilots, to scaling proven value.
- **Why it exists / what problem it solves**: A single enterprise-wide rollout expands before governance, evidence, and skills are ready. Stages create learning gates before more money and risk are committed.
- **Keywords**: foundation, pilot, scale, readiness, adoption
- **Related concepts**: [[business-outcome-aligned-ai-use-case-selection]], [[llm-observability]], [[metric-driven-llm-optimization]], [[agent-centered-workflow-redesign]], [[agentic-workflow-complexity-ladder]]
- **Depth**: 2/4
- **Last updated**: 2026-09-26
- **Source**: The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide

## Summary

Think of AI adoption as crossing three bridges. First, align leaders, governance, use cases, and people. Next, run a small pilot that can show real evidence. Only then spread the practices, skills, and support that worked. A technical improvement inside one AI system is useful, but this model decides when the organization itself is ready to advance.

## Example

A retailer first sets data rules and selects an internal inventory-help pilot. It measures whether the pilot improves work for eight weeks. After the results are credible, it trains other teams and creates shared support instead of copying the tool everywhere immediately.
