# Cross-Functional AI Steering Committee

- **One-sentence definition**: A cross-functional AI steering committee brings executive authority together with business, technology, finance, legal, and compliance expertise to make AI decisions.
- **Why it exists / what problem it solves**: AI changes budgets, workflows, data access, risk, and accountability at once. One team cannot responsibly make every trade-off alone.
- **Keywords**: sponsor, governance, finance, legal, accountability
- **Related concepts**: [[domain-owned-semantic-conventions]]
- **Depth**: 2/4
- **Last updated**: 2026-09-26
- **Source**: The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide

## Summary

An AI steering committee is a small group with the authority and viewpoints needed to unblock and govern change. An executive sponsor removes obstacles; functional leaders explain real work; technology leaders assess implementation; finance tracks investment; and legal and compliance set boundaries. The point is not another meeting. It is making ownership for choices that cross departments explicit.

## Example

A sales team proposes an AI assistant that uses customer data. The steering committee checks the business case, confirms technical access, budgets the work, and asks legal whether the proposed data handling is permitted before the pilot begins.
