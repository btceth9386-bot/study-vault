# Enterprise AI Change-Champion Network

- **One-sentence definition**: A change-champion network equips respected managers, technical experts, early adopters, and constructive skeptics to help their local teams adopt AI.
- **Why it exists / what problem it solves**: Leaders cannot see every workflow problem or create peer trust. Local champions make the change credible and surface real objections.
- **Keywords**: champions, peers, adoption, mentorship, feedback
- **Related concepts**: [[learned-operational-knowledge-files]]
- **Depth**: 2/4
- **Last updated**: 2026-09-26
- **Source**: The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide

## Summary

People usually learn a new way of working from someone they trust nearby. Champions translate a broad AI strategy into examples that fit a team, and skeptics reveal problems before they become quiet resistance. Give those people training, access to leaders, and visible recognition so useful practices can spread through existing relationships.

## Example

When one department struggles to use an AI assistant, a successful colleague runs a short peer session using that department's real tasks and records the questions that still block adoption.
