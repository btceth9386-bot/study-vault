# Role-Based AI Capability Building

- **One-sentence definition**: Role-based AI capability building gives executives, managers, and power users different learning paths and validates competence through real work.
- **Why it exists / what problem it solves**: One training program either overwhelms decision-makers or leaves practitioners unable to apply the tools.
- **Keywords**: executives, managers, power users, proficiency, practice
- **Related concepts**: [[agent-skills-as-procedural-memory]], [[human-agent-work-redesign-metrics]]
- **Depth**: 2/4
- **Last updated**: 2026-09-26
- **Source**: The Enterprise AI Transformation Guide

## Summary

Executives need enough context to make investment decisions, managers need to translate strategy into daily work, and power users need deeper hands-on skills. Good training tests a real task instead of attendance or multiple-choice recall. That makes capability visible and useful rather than merely completed.

## Example

A manager completes training by redesigning one team workflow with appropriate AI controls, while a power user demonstrates how to diagnose a failed prompt and improve it.
